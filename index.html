About Hiraz • Bijbehara

MADE FOR CRAVINGS.
BUILT FOR good times.

Not a fancy fine-dine. A loud, warm, delicious adda for pizza nights, crispy bites & late mocktails.

Hiraz Foods sits near Govt Girls Higher Secondary School, Zirpara — wooden booths, red beams, big pendant lights and the smell of fresh crust in the air. Students, families, friends and travellers stop here for familiar favourites done right: cheesy pizzas, juicy burgers, hot & crispy chicken, rolls, momos, biryani, fried rice, coffee and coolers.
